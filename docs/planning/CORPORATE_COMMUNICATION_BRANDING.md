# Corporate communication branding

For all employee-, HR-, and corporate-facing communication, use **My SmartHealth** as the product and wellness-service brand.

- Use **My SmartHealth** in email and WhatsApp copy, sender display names, template headers and footers, and corporate-facing documents.
- **Mediczy** is the underlying software/platform name. Do not present it in corporate communications.
- Technical URLs, system headers, service identifiers, and email-domain addresses may retain `mediqzy` where they are infrastructure rather than visible branding.

Applies to corporate AHC, benefits, onboarding, reminders, and HR communications.
