## 6. Offline & Ground Marketing

### Clinic & Hospital Tie-up Program

**What Mediqzy offers clinics:**
- Co-branded standee + prescription pad inserts ("Dr. [Name] recommends Mediqzy for your family's ongoing health")
- ₹100-200 per activated patient credited to clinic account
- Monthly top-referring clinic: featured on Mediqzy app as "Partner Clinic"
- Free Mediqzy Diamond for the doctor's own family

**Process:**
1. Area sales rep visits 5-10 clinics/week
2. Demo the app in 10 minutes
3. Leave onboarding kit (QR cards + pamphlet + enrolment form)
4. Clinic staff trained to offer Mediqzy at checkout: "Doctor has recommended this for follow-up care"
5. Track via clinic-specific referral code

### Pharmacy Tie-up Program

India has 9 lakh+ pharmacies. A customer buying medicines is a pre-qualified health plan prospect.

**Pharmacy offer:**
- Counter card: "Ask us about Mediqzy — save 20% on all medicines + free doctor on call"
- Pharmacist gets ₹100 per Silver+ activation, ₹250 per Gold+ activation
- Trial activation can happen right at the counter (pharmacist scans QR, customer gets 3-month Basic free, pharmacist earns on upgrade)

### Housing Society / RWA Activation

- Partner with 5-10 RWAs per city
- Conduct free "Family Health Camp" (basic vitals check, BMI, BP) — convert attendees to Basic trial
- Offer society-wide Group Gold rate: ₹349/month (special rate for 20+ members from same society)
- Resident welfare association gets ₹500/month as "health partner" fee for facilitating

### Corporate Events & HR Summits

- Sponsor 2-3 HR events/quarter with a booth
- Offer: "Enrol 10 employees today, first month free for all"
- Leave behind: ROI calculator showing cost of employee sick days vs Mediqzy Corporate cost

## 7. Corporate B2B Sales Strategy

### Sales Process (5 Steps)

```
Step 1 — IDENTIFY
  → LinkedIn Sales Navigator: filter HR Manager/Head, 50-500 employees, India
  → Target sectors: IT services, logistics, retail, hospitality, manufacturing
  → Ideal prospect: company without group mediclaim OR with expensive TPA

Step 2 — OUTREACH
  → LinkedIn InMail + cold email (personalised, 3-line pitch)
  → Subject: "Free 1-month health pilot for your team"
  → No deck in first message. Just: "10 employees, zero cost, 30 days"

Step 3 — DEMO (30 minutes)
  → Show employee app + HR dashboard
  → Key demo points: teleconsult in 2 min, family coverage, AI health reports

Step 4 — PILOT
  → 10-employee free trial, 30 days
```

### Commission Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **No commission on discounted price** — Commission calculated on standard face price only. Discount comes out of agent's allocation, not Mediqzy's margin.
2. **No commission until payment clears** — 30-day cooling-off window must pass.
3. **Annual billing clawback** — Cancel within 3 months = 50% commission clawback from next cycle.
4. **Silver commission held 90 days** — Prevents churn gaming on low-margin plan.
5. **Commission not stackable** — An activation can only be attributed to one channel. First-touch wins (UTM / agent code).

## 9. Agency Onboarding Process

### Who Is a Mediqzy Agency Partner?

An individual or firm that:
- Recruits and manages a team of 3+ field agents
- Has geographic territory (district / city / zone)
- Commits to minimum 30 activations/month
- Has a GST registration (or willingness to get one)

---

### Onboarding in 5 Steps

#### Step 1 — Application & Screening (Day 1-3)
- Application via Mediqzy Partner Portal (or WhatsApp form for smaller partners)
- Required info: Name, location, GST/PAN, prior experience in insurance/health/finance/FMCG sales
- Screening call: 20-minute video call with Mediqzy channel manager
- Background check: Basic verification (not criminal, no history of misrepresentation)
- **Approval/rejection within 3 working days**

#### Step 2 — Agreement Signing (Day 3-5)
Partner signs:
- Channel Partner Agreement (CPA)
- Commission schedule annexure
- Code of Conduct (no misrepresentation of benefits, no selling to minors, no fake activations)
- Data protection addendum (handles customer PII — DPDPA compliant)
- Non-compete clause: cannot represent directly competing health membership platforms

Digital signature via DigiSign / Leegality. No physical stamp paper required for partners below ₹50L annual commission.

#### Step 3 — Training & Certification (Day 5-10)
All agency partners and their agents must complete:

| Module | Duration | Format | Pass Mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mediqzy Product Knowledge | 2 hours | Video + quiz | 80% |
| Plan Comparison & Objection Handling | 1 hour | Video + roleplay | 80% |
| Compliance & Ethics | 30 min | Video | 100% (mandatory) |
| App Demo & Enrolment Walkthrough | 1 hour | Screen recording | Practical |
| Corporate B2B Sales (if applicable) | 2 hours | Live webinar | 75% |

- Training hosted on a simple LMS (can use Teachable / Thinkific / custom portal)
- Certificate issued on passing all modules: "Mediqzy Certified Partner"
- Refresher training: Quarterly (mandatory for new products/plans)

#### Step 4 — Kit & Access (Day 10)
On certification, agency partner receives:

**Digital:**
- Agency Partner Dashboard login (activation tracking, commission ledger, payout history)
- Unique agency referral code (all sub-agents get codes prefixed with agency code)
- Digital marketing kit: product brochures (PDF), WhatsApp-ready images, plan comparison sheets, video scripts
- Co-branded materials template (editable Canva templates with agency name/logo)

**Physical (courier within 7 days):**
- 50 Mediqzy membership brochures (tri-fold, full colour)
- 10 clinic/pharmacy standee cards (A5, laminated)
- 5 roll-up standee designs (for events — partner arranges printing locally)
- Welcome letter + agency partner ID card

#### Step 5 — Go-Live & First Month Support (Day 10-40)
- Dedicated Channel Manager assigned (1 CM handles up to 20 agencies)
- WhatsApp group: Agency + Channel Manager for real-time support
- Weekly check-in call in Month 1 (fortnightly from Month 2)
- First activation within 30 days of go-live is required to maintain partner status
- **Month 1 target: 10 activations minimum**

### Agency Termination Conditions

Agency agreement is terminated with 30 days notice if:
- Zero activations for 60 consecutive days
- Code of conduct violation (misrepresentation, fake activations, data misuse)
- Regulatory breach (selling insurance add-on plans without disclosing terms)
- Competing platform representation without written approval

On termination: agent codes deactivated immediately. Pending commissions (earned, not yet paid) are honoured. Future renewal commissions are forfeited.
