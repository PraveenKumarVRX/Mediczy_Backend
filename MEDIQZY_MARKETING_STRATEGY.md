## 1. Brand Positioning

### Tagline Options (A/B Test)
- **"Your Family's Health. All in One Place."** ← Recommended primary
- "Healthcare that comes to you"
- "India's first complete health membership"

### Positioning Statement
> SmartHealth is India's most complete health membership — combining unlimited GP consultations, annual health checkups, pharmacy benefits, lab discounts, optional health/accident insurance, VitalTrack remote monitoring wearables, and clinical disease management programs, starting at ₹1,499/year. Unlike teleconsult-only apps, SmartHealth is a modular, full-stack health OS: pick your Base plan, add Insurance (Plus), add a VitalTrack device (Pro), and stack on Clinical Riders for diabetes, cardiac, weight, elderly, or mental wellness care.

### Competitive Differentiation (Own These)
| Axis | Our Position |
|---|---|
| **Price** | 75% cheaper than Practo Plus for comparable or better benefits |
| **Completeness** | Only plan with pharmacy + checkup + teleconsult + insurance + wearable in one stack |
| **Family architecture** | Only platform with a 6-member (grandparents-included) family plan |
| **Insurance** | Bundled health + super top-up + accident insurance — no separate policy needed |
| **Wearables** | VitalTrack: smart band → smartwatch ladder included in Pro plans; clinical upgrade to glucose watch. Your doctor sees you every day — dedicated care team monitors vitals 24×7, flags issues before you feel them |
| **Clinical Programs** | 5 disease-specific riders (Diabetes, Cardiac, Weight, Elderly, Mental Wellness) — stackable on any plan |
| **AI** | Built-in AI health chat, HRA, AI Scribe, activity tracker — not bolted on |

### Promotional Hook Plan
> **Gold (₹2,999/yr) is the primary acquisition hook** — promoted aggressively across all channels as the mass-market entry point. Pitch: *"Less than ₹250/month. Unlimited GP + annual checkup + pharmacy discount. Two doctor visits and it pays for itself."* All other plans (Gold+, Gold Pro, riders) are the upsell story that follows.

### Wellness-First Promotion Principle
Lead all creative and agent pitches with **positive wellness benefits** — never open with doctor consultation (reactive, negative-emotion trigger). Promotion hierarchy:

| Priority | Service | Emotion | Hook Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | Annual health checkup | Aspiration — "know your body" | "Know your numbers before they know you" |
| 2nd | Pharmacy discount | Savings — tangible, immediate | "Save ₹3,000/year on medicines your family already buys" |
| 3rd | Lab tests | Convenience | "Book from home, get reports on your phone" |
| 4th | VitalTrack wearable | Empowerment — "see your health daily" | "Your doctor watching over you, every single day" |
| 5th | Doctor consultation | Safety net — close, don't open with it | "And when you need a doctor — unlimited, zero wait, free" |

> Doctor consultation is the **bonus** the prospect didn't expect, not the reason they buy. People don't want to need a doctor — they want to be well. **Wellness hooks, consultation retains.**

## 6. Offline & Ground Marketing

### Clinic & Hospital Tie-up Program

**What SmartHealth offers clinics:**
- Co-branded standee + prescription pad inserts ("Dr. [Name] recommends SmartHealth for your family's ongoing health")
- ₹100-200 per activated patient credited to clinic account
- Monthly top-referring clinic: featured on SmartHealth app as "Partner Clinic"
- Free SmartHealth Diamond for the doctor's own family

**Process:**
1. Area sales rep visits 5-10 clinics/week
2. Demo the app in 10 minutes
3. Leave onboarding kit (QR cards + pamphlet + enrolment form)
4. Clinic staff trained to offer SmartHealth at checkout: "Doctor has recommended this for follow-up care"
5. Track via clinic-specific referral code

### Pharmacy Tie-up Program

India has 9 lakh+ pharmacies. A customer buying medicines is a pre-qualified health plan prospect.

**Pharmacy offer:**
- Counter card: "Ask us about SmartHealth — save 20% on all medicines + free doctor on call"
- Pharmacist gets ₹100 per Silver+ activation, ₹250 per Gold+ activation
- Trial activation can happen right at the counter (pharmacist scans QR, customer gets 3-month Basic free, pharmacist earns on upgrade)

### Housing Society / RWA Activation

- Partner with 5-10 RWAs per city
- Conduct free "Family Health Camp" (basic vitals check, BMI, BP) — convert attendees to Basic trial
- Offer society-wide Group Gold rate: ₹349/month (special rate for 20+ members from same society)
- Resident welfare association gets ₹500/month as "health partner" fee for facilitating

### Corporate Events & HR Summits

- Sponsor 2-3 HR events/quarter with a booth
- Offer: "Enrol 10 employees today, first month free for all"
- Leave behind: ROI calculator showing cost of employee sick days vs SmartHealth Corporate cost

### Commission Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **No commission on discounted price** — Commission calculated on standard face price only. Discount comes out of agent's allocation, not SmartHealth's margin.
2. **No commission until payment clears** — 30-day cooling-off window must pass.
3. **Annual billing clawback** — Cancel within 3 months = 50% commission clawback from next cycle.
4. **Silver commission held 90 days** — Prevents churn gaming on low-margin plan.
5. **Commission not stackable** — An activation can only be attributed to one channel. First-touch wins (UTM / agent code).

## 9. Agency Onboarding Process

### Who Is a SmartHealth Agency Partner?

An individual or firm that:
- Recruits and manages a team of 3+ field agents
- Has geographic territory (district / city / zone)
- Commits to minimum 30 activations/month
- Has a GST registration (or willingness to get one)

---

### Onboarding in 5 Steps

#### Step 1 — Application & Screening (Day 1-3)
- Application via SmartHealth Partner Portal (or WhatsApp form for smaller partners)
- Required info: Name, location, GST/PAN, prior experience in insurance/health/finance/FMCG sales
- Screening call: 20-minute video call with SmartHealth channel manager
- Background check: Basic verification (not criminal, no history of misrepresentation)
- **Approval/rejection within 3 working days**

#### Step 2 — Agreement Signing (Day 3-5)
Partner signs:
- Channel Partner Agreement (CPA)
- Commission schedule annexure
- Code of Conduct (no misrepresentation of benefits, no selling to minors, no fake activations)
- Data protection addendum (handles customer PII — DPDPA compliant)
- Non-compete clause: cannot represent directly competing health membership platforms

Digital signature via DigiSign / Leegality. No physical stamp paper required for partners below ₹50L annual commission.

#### Step 3 — Training & Certification (Day 5-10)
All agency partners and their agents must complete:

| Module | Duration | Format | Pass Mark |
|---|---|---|---|
| SmartHealth Product Knowledge | 2 hours | Video + quiz | 80% |
| Plan Comparison & Objection Handling | 1 hour | Video + roleplay | 80% |
| Compliance & Ethics | 30 min | Video | 100% (mandatory) |
| App Demo & Enrolment Walkthrough | 1 hour | Screen recording | Practical |
| Corporate B2B Sales (if applicable) | 2 hours | Live webinar | 75% |

- Training hosted on a simple LMS (can use Teachable / Thinkific / custom portal)
- Certificate issued on passing all modules: "SmartHealth Certified Partner"
- Refresher training: Quarterly (mandatory for new products/plans)

#### Step 4 — Kit & Access (Day 10)
On certification, agency partner receives:

**Digital:**
- Agency Partner Dashboard login (activation tracking, commission ledger, payout history)
- Unique agency referral code (all sub-agents get codes prefixed with agency code)
- Digital marketing kit: product brochures (PDF), WhatsApp-ready images, plan comparison sheets, video scripts
- Co-branded materials template (editable Canva templates with agency name/logo)

**Physical (courier within 7 days):**
